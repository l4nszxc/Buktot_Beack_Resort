Destination Background

About Buktot Beach Resort

A tranquil coastal sanctuary in Southern Oriental Mindoro offering natural peace, cultural heritage, and unspoiled seascapes.

Cebuano Origin

"Buktot" translates to "hunchback", named after the distinctive hunched hill overlooking the shoreline.

Where Pristine Nature Remains Unspoiled

Nestled quietly along the eastern coastline of Mansalay in Barangay Manaul, Oriental Mindoro, Buktot Beach is renowned as one of the province's best-kept secrets. Far from the commercial rush and bustling crowds of mainstream resorts, Buktot boasts a powdery ivory shoreline bathed by crystal-clear, calm coastal waters.

Our Vision

To serve as a premier model of sustainable, community-supported eco-tourism that protects Mindoro's marine biodiversity.

Our Mission

Provide tourists with authentic, relaxing, and memorable coastal leisure while empowering local Mangyan and Mindoreño coastal residents.

Unique Characteristics & Tourism Value:
  • Distinctive Geological Boulders: The right edge of the cove presents towering boulders and unique violet-tinted rock formations.
  • Peaceful & Low-Density Tourism: Strict nature-focused atmosphere that preserves authentic quietude for guests and campers.
  • Target Market: Nature lovers, barkada backpackers, photographers, eco-tourists, and families seeking an uncommercialized beach sanctuary.

Getting There & Logistics

Destination & Travel Information

Everything you need to know about traveling to Brgy. Manaul, Mansalay, Oriental Mindoro.

How to Get to Buktot Beach

Step 1: Manila to Batangas Pier to Calapan

Take a bus from Buendia/Cubao to Batangas Port (2 hrs), then take a FastCat or RORO ferry to Calapan Port, Oriental Mindoro (1.5 to 2 hrs).

Step 2: Calapan to Mansalay Town Proper

From Calapan Grand Terminal, board an air-conditioned UV Express van or provincial bus bound for Roxas/Mansalay/Bulalacao. Travel time: approx. 3.5 to 4 hours along the Strong Republic Nautical Highway.

Step 3: Mansalay Town Proper to Brgy. Manaul (Buktot Beach)

From Mansalay Public Market / Town Plaza, hire a local tricycle or habal-habal motorcycle to Buktot Beach in Brgy. Manaul. Travel time is around 25 to 35 minutes along semi-rough scenic coastal roads.

Pro Tip: Agree on a round-trip or pick-up schedule with your tricycle driver, as public rides are infrequent at the beach area in the late afternoon.

Responsible Tourism

Tourist Guide & Travel Reminders

Help us preserve Buktot Beach for future generations with these essential visitor tips.

Best Time to Visit

The dry season from November to May offers the calmest seas and clearest waters. Early mornings (7:00 AM - 11:00 AM) are ideal for tranquil swimming.

What to Bring

Reef-safe sunscreen, aqua shoes (for rock exploration), drinking water, picnic snacks, trash bags, power banks, and cash (no ATMs on the beach).

Eco Guidelines

"Leave No Trace." Pack out whatever you pack in. Do not collect sand, coral, or seashells. Single-use plastic is strictly regulated.

Local Culture & Respect

Mansalay is home to the indigenous Hanunuo Mangyan people. Treat locals with warmth and respect, and maintain modest beachwear in public town areas.
